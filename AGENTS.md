# CityMETER Office — owner preferences

Set 2026-11-05 (release 0.1.0) on the owner request “จากที่เราทำ demo ของ CityMETER: Hotel มาแล้ว ใน https://montri-th.github.io/hotel ขอให้ทำแบบเดียวกันให้กับ CityMETER: Office แล้ว publish ที่ https://github.com/montri-th/office โดยใช้ข้อมูลจาก [the office research master] ซึ่งรวบรวมมาจาก งานเดิมใน production ที่เป็น CityMETER: Office มาปรับปรุง … update registry sheet”, with the owner's choice of the same day to read “CityMETER Office — Venue, Company & Building Research Master 2026-10-24” and to update the Office row of the CityMETER Dataset Registry (R01_DATASETS). Code base: montri-th/hotel @177e592 (CityMETER · Hotel 0.3.6); every Hotel rule below that still applies is carried over unchanged, the hotel-only parts (rooms, brands and chains, logos, current names, stay sites, retail context) are not.

- Apply current LDS 0.9.7 (v0.9.7-owner.1, standalone-0.9.7-r1, color-srgb-10) and preserve the map-first CityMETER layout. Colours come only from `assets/theme.css`, `js/scales.js` and `js/cues.js`; never approximate a value by eye. Analytical colours are the same HEX on both themes (`count` for offices, `price` for the median asking rent; all 41 samples by rank — owner standing rule); EVID-05 map treatments use the `dataState` tokens. `npm run check:lds -- --lds <base>` compares every shipped value with the source file.
- Read `PRODUCT_STATEMENT.md` before changing what the first screen argues. The question is “สำนักงานกระจุกอยู่ตรงไหน ค่าเช่าที่ประกาศเท่าไร และใกล้รถไฟฟ้าแค่ไหน”.
- Measure is a contract (`js/model.js#METRICS`, `rowValue`, `valueOf`): offices (“แห่ง”, Office IDs, `count`) by default; the median of dated starting asking rents (“ค่าเช่าที่ประกาศ”, THB/m²/month, `price`) on the switch, only from `RENT_MIN_N` = 5 rents — fewer is `no_data` (hatch, “< 5 ราคา”), no office is `out_of_scope`. An office record is never called a building; a rent is never called a market, contract, achieved or average rent; never call a figure revenue, demand, a market share, a market size or a density. Undated rents and legacy production prices are shown per office only.
- Aha first, in 3D: the first paint is the `city` preset framing the districts with the most offices in the province that holds the most at district level (≤ 3; a tie at the cut joins while the focus stays ≤ 4 and leaves a district out — `js/area-insight.js`), the share of the province's offices with numerator and denominator, the rail comparison as two bars (both bases), the outside finding; on the rent measure the districts with the highest median (each with its n), the province's median, medians near rail and farther (each with its n). Never focus every district that holds the measure. Snapshot numbers in the handoff JSON are fixtures, never UI constants.
- Membership is the district matched at build time from the row's own data (`row.admin`: name · point · province · conflict · none). Never re-assign by point-in-polygon on display coordinates or by an office name at runtime.
- One row = one Office ID; the sheet's aggregate references with child rows are kept out (`meta.excluded`). Categories (`office_supply_model`) and evidence (`qualification_status`) come from the sheet; the build stops on a value it has no rule for. Link and classification changes are made in the research sheet, then re-read and rebuilt — never patched in `data/`.
- Organisations join by id (`19_ORGANIZATION_ROLES` → `18_ORGANIZATIONS`); broker listings, unverified brand affiliations and co-marketing links are not organisations of the office; a role is a source claim, never a tenancy or ownership check.
- Context comes from datasets of the same registry, joined by id with the commit and SHA-256 recorded (`meta.context`): CityMETER · Transit stations. Every distance is a straight line between reference points — the distance pictogram, “ระยะตรง” in titles.
- Rent comparison set (`compsetOf`): offices with an asking rent within 1 km straight line, nearest first, every row of the dataset; headed “ค่าเช่ารอบ ๆ”, never “คู่แข่ง” or a valuation.
- No caveat on screen (owner rule of Hotel 0.3.1, “ไม่มี caveats เลย”): exactness comes from the names (“ค่าเช่าที่ประกาศ”, “ราคาที่บันทึก (เดิม)”, “ค่าเช่ากลาง”), the n of every median, the numerator and denominator of every share, and one provenance line per view (dataset · source · date · boundary, then “วิธีนับ” → `DATA_METHOD.md`). Limits live in `DATA_METHOD.md` only (`tests/views.test.js`).
- Building view and MapLibre: follow the Hotel 0.3.6 contracts unchanged (`js/shell/camera.js`, `js/massing.js`, `js/shell/venue-xray.js`, `js/shell/maplibre-guard.js` with MapLibre pinned at 5.6.2, `GEOJSON_MAXZOOM`, building units by the 15 % rule). An office with a point opens the two-point view of its building (or the nearest drawn building within 300 m, then 800 m) and switches the map to 3D; the nearest building is a camera choice only.
- One symbol always represents one office with a point, at one size for every office (a record can be a few floors or a tower); never cluster or aggregate. Names near the middle of the view rank by floors and asking-rent percentiles (`row.importance`, ranking only).
- Outbound actions (`js/ask.js`): ถาม Google AI as a plain Google Search with the question of the view, Google Maps, Street View only with a point. Nothing from Google's answers enters the data.
- Breadcrumb is ประเทศไทย › จังหวัด › เขต/อำเภอ with production codes and deep links `/v3/citymeter/{code}/D/{district}?d=office`.
- Privacy and publication: never copy addresses, telephone numbers, researcher notes, ownership notes, company location candidates, legal registration ids, the private repository's links or raw ids (`meta.excludedFields`); source links are public https pages only. Never commit the research sheet or its tab files, private Drive/Sheet URLs, credentials or the LDS library. Never put Landometer prices, contracts, proposals or pipeline in this repository. Review Thai at desktop and 393 px in light and dark before claiming done; local renders are not proof of a live release — run `npm run verify:live` and walk the live site.
- Releases bump one cache tag `?v=YYYYMMDD-rN` on every entry in `index.html` and, through the import map, on every ES module, together with `package.json` version, `design-intent.json` and the handoff JSON `productVersion` (`tests/release.test.js`).

## Discovery and dataset group (owner decision 2026-11-08)

- Every page carries `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`. The owner does not want search engines to index the demos while developers keep building on them: “ไม่ต้องการให้เสิช engine เข้ามาดูเดโม แต่อยากให้ dev นำ demo บน github ไป vibe coding ต่อได้ง่าย คือ SEO อันไหนจัดการได้จัดการไปเลย แต่ยังไม่ต้องให้ crawler เข้ามา index”. Keep the rest of the SEO metadata ready for production, do not block crawlers in a robots.txt (they must be able to read the noindex), add no sitemap, and never point `rel="canonical"` at landometer.com from a noindex page. `tests/discovery-and-group.test.js` guards this.
- The panel eyebrow `CITYMETER · LAND` follows the dataset registry (`R01_DATASETS.dataset_group`; owner: “ใช้ตามทะเบียนทั้งหมด”). This dataset is LAND in the registry read on 2026-11-08; change the label only when the registry changes.
