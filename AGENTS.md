Code base: montri-th/hotel @177e592 (CityMETER · Hotel 0.3.6); every Hotel rule below that still applies is carried over unchanged, the hotel-only parts (rooms, brands and chains, logos, current names, stay sites, retail context) are not.

- Apply current LDS 0.9.7 (v0.9.7-owner.1, standalone-0.9.7-r1, color-srgb-10) and preserve the map-first CityMETER layout. Colours come only from `assets/theme.css`, `js/scales.js` and `js/cues.js`; never approximate a value by eye. - Read `PRODUCT_STATEMENT.md` before changing what the first screen argues. The question is “สำนักงานกระจุกอยู่ตรงไหน ค่าเช่าที่ประกาศเท่าไร และใกล้รถไฟฟ้าแค่ไหน”.
- Measure is a contract (`js/model.js#METRICS`, `rowValue`, `valueOf`): offices (“แห่ง”, Office IDs, `count`) by default; the median of dated starting asking rents (“ค่าเช่าที่ประกาศ”, THB/m²/month, `price`) on the switch, only from `RENT_MIN_N` = 5 rents — fewer is `no_data` (hatch, “< 5 ราคา”), no office is `out_of_scope`. An office record is never called a building; a rent is never called a market, contract, achieved or average rent; never call a figure revenue, demand, a market share, a market size or a density. Undated rents and legacy production prices are shown per office only.
- Aha first, in 3D: the first paint is the `city` preset framing the districts with the most offices in the province that holds the most at district level (≤ 3; a tie at the cut joins while the focus stays ≤ 4 and leaves a district out — `js/area-insight.js`), the share of the province's offices with numerator and denominator, the rail comparison as two bars (both bases), the outside finding; on the rent measure the districts with the highest median (each with its n), the province's median, medians near rail and farther (each with its n). Never focus every district that holds the measure. Snapshot numbers in the handoff JSON are fixtures, never UI constants.
- Membership is the district matched at build time from the row's own data (`row.admin`: name · point · province · conflict · none). Never re-assign by point-in-polygon on display coordinates or by an office name at runtime.
- One row = one Office ID; the sheet's aggregate references with child rows are kept out (`meta.excluded`). Categories (`office_supply_model`) and evidence (`qualification_status`) come from the sheet; the build stops on a value it has no rule for. Link and classification changes are made in the research sheet, then re-read and rebuilt — never patched in `data/`.
- Organisations join by id (`19_ORGANIZATION_ROLES` → `18_ORGANIZATIONS`); broker listings, unverified brand affiliations and co-marketing links are not organisations of the office; a role is a source claim, never a tenancy or ownership check.
- Context comes from datasets of the same registry, joined by id with the commit and SHA-256 recorded (`meta.context`): CityMETER · Transit stations. Every distance is a straight line between reference points — the distance pictogram, “ระยะตรง” in titles.
- Rent comparison set (`compsetOf`): offices with an asking rent within 1 km straight line, nearest first, every row of the dataset; headed “ค่าเช่ารอบ ๆ”, never “คู่แข่ง” or a valuation.
- No caveat on screen (owner rule of Hotel 0.3.1, “ไม่มี caveats เลย”): exactness comes from the names (“ค่าเช่าที่ประกาศ”, “ราคาที่บันทึก (เดิม)”, “ค่าเช่ากลาง”), the n of every median, the numerator and denominator of every share, and one provenance line per view (dataset · source · date · boundary, then “วิธีนับ” → `DATA_METHOD.md`). Limits live in `DATA_METHOD.md` only (`tests/views.test.js`).
- Building view and MapLibre: follow the Hotel 0.3.6 contracts unchanged (`js/shell/camera.js`, `js/massing.js`, `js/shell/venue-xray.js`, `js/shell/maplibre-guard.js` with MapLibre pinned at 5.6.2, `GEOJSON_MAXZOOM`, building units by the 15 % rule). - One symbol always represents one office with a point, at one size for every office (a record can be a few floors or a tower); never cluster or aggregate. Names near the middle of the view rank by floors and asking-rent percentiles (`row.importance`, ranking only).
- Outbound actions (`js/ask.js`): ถาม Google AI as a plain Google Search with the question of the view, Google Maps, Street View only with a point. Nothing from Google's answers enters the data.
- Breadcrumb is ประเทศไทย › จังหวัด › เขต/อำเภอ with production codes and deep links `/v3/citymeter/{code}/D/{district}?d=office`.
- Privacy and publication: never copy addresses, telephone numbers, researcher notes, ownership notes, company location candidates, legal registration ids, the private repository's links or raw ids (`meta.excludedFields`); source links are public https pages only. Never commit the research sheet or its tab files, private Drive/Sheet URLs, credentials or the LDS library. Never put Landometer prices, contracts, proposals or pipeline in this repository. Review Thai at desktop and 393 px in light and dark before claiming done; local renders are not proof of a live release — run `npm run verify:live` and walk the live site.
- Releases bump one cache tag `?v=YYYYMMDD-rN` on every entry in `index.html` and, through the import map, on every ES module, together with `package.json` version, `design-intent.json` and the handoff JSON `productVersion` (`tests/release.test.js`).

## Search — names found the way people type them (owner 2026-11-12)

- Since release 0.6.0 (citymeter-shell 0.7.0; owner 2026-11-12 12:05, VERBATIM: “ศึกษาแนวคิดเรื่อง Fuzzy search แล้ว implement กับ demo web ให้ได้ user benefit สูงสุดตาม user intent ในขณะนั้น และตาม product statement”; 13:15 “รวม university ไว้ด้วยกันเลย”) the search box lists names instead of filtering while it is typed in. `js/shell/fuzzy.js` is the matcher — the method of the CityMETER area-search brief (02-CityMETER__fuzzy-search-tech-brief v1, 19 Oct 2026, revised 20 Oct), moved from PostgreSQL to the browser because a demo is a static site: a name as typed, without its tone marks, by letters that sound alike, with its lead word (จ. อ. เขต) or the place it is in, typed on the other keyboard layout, or one or two keys off; the last three are guesses, made only when the sure steps find little. `js/shell/search.js` is the list under the box (a WAI-ARIA combobox in the flow of the panel) and `js/find.js` gives it this demo's names: provinces, เขต/อำเภอ, rail stations, organisations, office types and offices (an office also by its other name, its aliases and its project).
- Intent decides the action: a place opens its level, a station the offices round it, an organisation or an office type its filter, an office its profile. Enter with nothing chosen applies the words as the text filter, as the box always did; when the words keep no row, Enter marks the first name found as typed and Enter again opens it — a row is never opened unmarked. The map and the panel do not change while the reader types.
- What is chosen is shown. A filter or words chosen from the list apply to the whole page: the place being looked at is left (the province stays — a province without the brand is a reading of that province) and the other filters that would leave the choice no row are lifted first, the fewest that do (`makeWay` → `filtersToLift`, js/shell/search.js). An item chosen always opens: the filters it does not pass are lifted (`makeWayFor` → `filtersAgainst`). The `filters` option of `installSearch` (js/app.js) names the filters that may be lifted — never a counting unit or a measure. The number on the list's last row is the rows the words keep with the filters that are on — or, when those leave them no row, with the ones that stay once the fewest are lifted, as choosing the row does (`rowsAfterWay`).
- A guess is never chosen for the reader (the brief's decision of 10 Oct 2026: “เลือกแถวให้อัตโนมัติเฉพาะเมื่อมีพื้นที่เดียวที่ชื่อตรง”). Only an entry that alone is written with the very name typed, or that name with its place, is marked ahead — never an office, and never an entry that only goes by the name once its head word is left out (“ภูเก็ต” is the province; เทศบาลนครภูเก็ต is found by it and is a row below).
- A row says what an entry is and where, in words: no number and no caveat. How sure a match is stands in words too — the tag “สะกดต่างจากที่พิมพ์”, the heading “ชื่อใกล้เคียง”.
- Nothing typed is stored or sent: no query log and no feedback button (the brief keeps both for after its PDPA review).
- Other names of a place are the four of `PROVINCE_AKA` (js/shell/fuzzy.js) and a district's name without เมือง. Who keeps a fuller alias list is an open decision of the brief: do not grow the list by hand. A name the data carries in one language is found in that language only.
- A new kind of entry needs its line in `searchNote` (js/find.js), its action in `openFound` (js/app.js) and, on an English page, the words of its line in js/lang-en.js (the `.find-where` context); `tests/find.test.js` checks them. The demo's own `#search` input stays the field of the applied text filter (hidden, read by the code as before); if js/shell/search.js does not load, the box filters as it always did.
- On a phone, while a name is typed the panel stands tall and the box comes to its top, so the list has room above the keyboard (the list's `onFocus` in js/app.js sets this demo's sheet to its full height and its `pick` brings it back to its resting height once a name is chosen, so the map shows the choice; `html.find-typing` and the box's `scroll-margin-top` are in navigation.css).
- Before a release, type in the box at 393 px and on a desktop, in Thai and in English: a name as written, one without its tone marks, one with a wrong key, one on the other keyboard layout, a text that is no name — and, with a filter on, a name of another kind (the filter gives way).
